# Tawseef Masud | Civil & Structural Engineering Portfolio

Second-year Civil & Structural Engineering student at the University of Leeds, currently on track for a 2:1. This public portfolio showcases selected university design projects, with an emphasis on Autodesk Revit, structural thinking, technical communication and collaborative design development.

> **Academic work:** These are coursework design proposals, not built projects or construction-ready engineering designs. Drawings and calculations have not been independently verified for real-world construction.

## Featured projects

### 1. St George's Field Pavilion — Concept Development & Revit Modelling
A partially open outdoor shelter concept designed to respond to St George's Field and provide shelter without becoming a fully enclosed building. The process included site analysis, precedent research, concept development, material research, structural considerations and technical communication.

**Skills developed:** Revit modelling and contextual views; plans, sections, elevations, details and 3D visualisation; comparing material and construction options; collaborative design reviews and presentation.

[Project notes](projects/01-st-georges-field-pavilion/README.md) · [Initial design board](assets/pavilion-board-1.jpg) · [Final design board](assets/pavilion-board-2.jpg)

### 2. St George's Field Boundary Barrier — Structural Design & Foundation Checks
A contemporary concept to replace a 20 m gap in the existing boundary wall. The design explored how to maintain a complete physical boundary while considering visual impact, durability, maintenance, buildability, wind loading and foundation bearing capacity.

**Skills developed:** Interpreting design constraints; comparing material options; communicating a concept through technical drawings; and applying foundation calculations and stability checks to an academic proposal.

[Project notes](projects/02-st-georges-field-vertical-barrier/README.md) · [Final design board](assets/vertical-barrier-board.jpg)

### 3. St George's Field Raised Walkway — Accessible Elevated Route
An elevated route concept connecting access points around St George's Field and the Sir William Henry Bragg Building. The scheme considered accessibility, site context, structural stability, material selection, connections and construction method.

**Skills developed:** Revit modelling; coordinated plans, sections, elevations and 3D views; conceptual structural detailing; and collaborative decision-making.

[Project notes](projects/03-st-georges-field-raised-walkway/README.md) · [Final design board](assets/raised-walkway-board.jpg)

## Technical skills explored

- **Autodesk Revit:** 3D modelling, contextual models, plans, sections, elevations, details and visualisations
- **Structural design fundamentals:** load paths, stability, connections and conceptual foundation checks
- **Design development:** site analysis, precedent research, concept evaluation and material selection
- **Construction awareness:** construction sequence, durability, maintenance and relative cost
- **Communication and teamwork:** technical drawing, presentation boards, design reviews and collaborative decisions
