# St George's Field Boundary Barrier
**Module:** CIVE1665 Integrated Design Project · Project 3  
**Location:** St George's Field, University of Leeds  
**Project type:** Group concept design with individual foundation calculation

## Overview
Developed a proposal for a contemporary barrier to replace a 20 m gap in the existing boundary wall. The brief required a complete barrier within a defined site zone and height range. The design had to consider visual impact, relative material cost, durability, maintenance, construction methods and stability above and below ground.

## Engineering considerations
The project explored how to express a contemporary design without simply copying the original masonry wall. Structural thinking included wind loading, overall stability, ground conditions and the relationship between the proposed barrier and its foundation. The academic work included a section detail and foundation calculation based on the bearing-capacity information stated in the brief.

## Skills developed
- Interpreting a constrained brief and translating it into design criteria.
- Comparing materials and construction approaches against cost, durability and aesthetics.
- Communicating design intent through plans, sections, elevations and 3D views.
- Applying foundational structural principles to an academic foundation-checking exercise.
- Coordinating design development and presentation with a group.

## Key learning
This project helped me understand that a visually distinctive design also needs a clear explanation of load path, foundation strategy, construction sequence and long-term maintenance.
