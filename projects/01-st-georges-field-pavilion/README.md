# St George's Field Pavilion
**Module:** CIVE1665 Integrated Design Project · Project 5  
**Location:** St George's Field, University of Leeds  
**Project type:** Group academic design proposal

## Overview
Developed a partially open pavilion concept intended to provide shelter from rain and wind while remaining connected to the surrounding landscape. The brief called for an accessible outdoor shelter responding to site context, views and local character, with consideration of aesthetics, materials, durability, construction and structural stability.

## Design development
The project progressed from site analysis and precedent research through individual concept ideas into a coordinated group proposal. The final concept explored a sculptural pavilion form linked to the story of St George, using the arrangement of panels to create a distinctive plan shape. The scheme considered seating, roof panels, glazed elements, columns, connections, foundations and a possible construction sequence.

## Revit and technical skills developed
- Developed familiarity with modelling a design in its Revit context and communicating the proposal in 3D.
- Worked with technical drawing outputs including a site plan, orthographic views, elevations, sections and construction details.
- Used digital views to communicate spatial form, material expression and key structural components.
- Built understanding of how coordinated drawings and views support explanation and review.

## Collaboration
Worked in a mixed group to discuss precedent research, compare concepts, combine ideas and progress a common proposal. This required sharing progress, responding to feedback, coordinating individual contributions and presenting the design as a coherent whole.

## Key learning
The project strengthened my understanding of how site context, user experience, material choices, constructability and structural arrangements influence one another. It also helped develop confidence in using Revit as a design communication tool.
